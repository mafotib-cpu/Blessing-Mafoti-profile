# AI Prompt Log

## 1. Prompt Used

I am a second-year Computer Information Systems student at Africa University in Zimbabwe. I am creating a personal one-page profile and mini-portfolio website using semantic HTML5.

Write a short and natural "About Me" paragraph for my website.

Requirements:
- Use simple English.
- Write in the first person.
- Mention that I am studying Computer Information Systems.
- Mention my interest in web development and information systems.
- Mention that I am interested in using technology to solve problems.
- Keep it between 60 and 90 words.
- Make it sound like a real university student, not like a company advertisement.
- Do not use complicated vocabulary.

Return only the About Me paragraph.

## 2. Raw AI Output

I am a Computer Information Systems student at Africa University with an interest in technology and how it can solve real-world problems. I am developing my skills in web development, information systems and other areas of computing. I enjoy learning how websites and information systems are designed and used to make work easier. My goal is to continue improving my technical skills and become a technology professional who can create useful solutions for people and organisations.

## 3. Final Edited Version

I am a Computer Information Systems student at Africa University. I am interested in technology, web development and information systems. I enjoy learning how technology can be used to solve problems and make people's everyday lives easier. I am developing my practical computer skills and learning how to create useful digital solutions. My goal is to continue improving my skills and become a technology professional.

## 4. Reflection

The AI helped me create an initial version of my About Me section. I edited the AI output to make it shorter, simpler and more suitable for my personal website. I also made sure that the final version reflects my own interests and goals rather than submitting the AI response without changes.
